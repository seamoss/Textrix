More things to do with themes

theme = "auto" follows your terminal: Textrix asks it for its background colour when it starts and takes theme_dark on a dark one, theme_light on a light one.

Give a room a theme of its own, so work looks like work: /theme here turbo, or a whole space with /theme space Work noir.

Make your own: /theme save mine writes the one you're on (with your [colors] changes) to a file you can edit and send to a friend, and /theme import mine.toml takes theirs. The README says how.

The accessible shelf is for colour blindness and low vision: colorblind and colorblind-light never put red against green (online is blue, away is orange), tritan is for blue-yellow colour blindness, and high-contrast is black and white with yellow and cyan.
