📟 What is this?!

Remember irssi? Numbered windows, a status bar that told you everything, /commands for absolutely everything, and you never touched the mouse (you can now, though). Textrix is that, for Matrix: chat with anyone on any Matrix server, from a terminal, with real end-to-end encryption, and no web browser pretending to be an app.

⭐ Features!!! ⭐

🔐 EncryptionEnd-to-end encrypted rooms, emoji verification, your recovery key, key backup. Even the local store is encrypted, with its key in your keychain.
🪟 Windowsirssi-style numbered windows, a window list, split panes you can drag to size and save as layouts, and a quick switcher (Ctrl+K) that finds anything you half-remember.
🌙 Background modeLike irssi in screen: textrix --daemon keeps you connected with no terminal, textrix comes back to it, /detach leaves it running.
⬆️ Upgradesbrew update && brew upgrade textrix and a running Textrix notices; /restart and you're on the new one, in the same window, with what you were typing.
🔎 SearchEvery room, encrypted ones too, up to 20,000 messages back in each, found instantly from an index kept encrypted on your disk. /search from:sam in:"Book Club" before:2026 pizza
🧵 Threads & spacesThreads open in their own windows. Spaces group your rooms.
🖼️ Pictures!Inline images, sharp in kitty, Ghostty, WezTerm and iTerm2, drawn in tiny blocks everywhere else, and a full-screen zoomable viewer. Drag a file onto the terminal to send it.
🎤 Voice & videoSee the waveform ▁▃▅▇▅▃▁, press z to hear it; videos open in your player.
🌈 CodeCode blocks in colour, even on a green-screen monitor (in shades of green).
🔔 BellsMentions, highlight words, per-room notification levels that sync with your other clients, a visual bell, and a mentions inbox.
🎨 ThemesFifty-one! Turbo Pascal blue, Procomm, C64, Amiga Workbench, Delphi 7, mIRC, a Game Boy, Ceefax, the blue screen of death, Hot Dog Stand, Solarized, Monokai, Dracula, Nord, Gruvbox, Catppuccin, synthwave (neon pink on a midnight-purple sky, totally rad), miami-heat, cemetery, vaporwave, disco, themes for colour blindness, an IBM 5151 framed in ASCII, and monochrome monitors in green, blue, red, amber or purple (on black, on 80s-airport blue, or Tron neon on pure black). Or auto, which matches your terminal. Make your own and send it to a friend, and give each room its own so work looks like work. There's also a secret way to get the green one. Ask a 1986 video game. 📊 Polls, stickers & emojiVote in Element's polls from the keyboard (results in little bars), start your own with /poll, see stickers, and use your image packs' custom emoji. NEW!
👤 Who's whoClick anyone's name for their picture, presence, role and verified devices, right beside the member list. NEW!
✍️ WritingCtrl+X opens your own editor for the long ones; /later 18:30 sends it while you're at dinner; nicks line up in a column like irssi's. NEW!
🏠 Home & galleryF4 shows everything waiting for you (mentions, unread rooms, invitations), a key away. /media lays out a room's pictures, videos and files as tiles to flick through. NEW!
🔗 Links & editsLinks show the page's title and picture, fetched by your homeserver so the site never sees you. Edited messages keep what they said before (i), ||spoilers|| stay hidden until revealed, and irssi's /exec -o uptime is back. NEW!
🏖️ Away logGo /away, come back to "while you were away: 3 mentions and 2 DMs", all in one window. And a status bar you arrange yourself. NEW!
📜 ScriptsLike irssi's Perl scripts, but in Rhai: drop a file in ~/.textrix/scripts to add commands, answer messages, run timers and put things in the status bar. The examples include dice.
⌨️ Commands/join /msg /me /away /ignore /lastlog /search /alias … and a few that aren't in /help.
